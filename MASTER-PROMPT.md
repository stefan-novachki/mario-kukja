The brief for this project

Build the same scroll walkthrough as `../villa-ambra` (the pencil plan that rises into a 3D
house, rooms turning real as you walk in, room cards, baked light, two quality versions), but
for my house, from the six design renders I sent: the street view, the interior living room,
the garden view, the aerial, the SketchUp massing model and the cut-away top view.

- The architecture, the structure and the design must correspond to the renders. Improve the
  house where it makes it better.
- Install everything needed for the best result (Blender for the light bake).
- Ready to deploy on Vercel.
